ResearchOur Longevity Model

Science, nature, and well-being for a fulfilling life.

We are an interdisciplinary team of researchers, therapists, farmers, and caregivers united by a conviction: the mental health of older adults can be transformed when science meets nature.

🌿

Therapeutic Nature

Nature as preventive and restorative medicine. Therapeutic forests, butterfly gardens, orchards, olive groves and green spaces designed to improve physical, emotional and cognitive health. .

🧬

Scientific Research

Evidence guides every intervention. We conduct research in healthy longevity, mental health, well-being and lifestyle medicine using rigorous and reproducible scientific methods.

🐾

Animal-Assisted Therapies

The human-animal bond as a therapeutic resource. Dogs, horses, butterflies and other animal interactions help improve emotional health, memory, motivation and quality of life.

🧘

Holistic Well-being

Caring for body, mind and purpose. We integrate physical activity, nutrition, social connection, mindfulness, education and healthy habits to promote longer, healthier lives.

Purposeful Longevity

Living longer is important. Living well with well-being, purpose, and connection is transformative.

The Valdes Armas model proposes a different way of understanding aging: more active, more human, and connected to the cycles of nature.
